NOVA Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Information used

NOVA may use the TikTok account identifier, authorization tokens, video metadata, and performance metrics needed to publish and analyze the owner's content.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate the owner's NOVA workflow, publish authorized content, maintain publishing status, and generate analytics summaries.

Sharing

NOVA does not sell personal information. Data is shared with TikTok only as required to authenticate the owner's account and publish content the owner approved.

Retention and deletion

The owner may disconnect TikTok access and request deletion of stored integration data by contacting adisorn.sangram.t@gmail.com.
